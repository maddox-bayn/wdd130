White Water Rafting Co.

Our mission is to provide unforgettable, adrenaline-pumping river adventures while maintaining the highest standard of outdoor safety. We believe the river teaches strength, unity, and a profound respect for nature's wildest elements.

History

Founded in 1998 by a small group of passionate river guides, White Water Rafting Co. started with just two rafts and a dream. Over the last nearly three decades, we have expanded our fleet to guide thousands of thrill-seekers down some of the most challenging and beautiful class IV and V rapids in the world. Our deep-seated dedication to ecotourism and sustainable outdoor exploration remains unchanged.
